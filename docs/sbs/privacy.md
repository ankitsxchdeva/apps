# SBS privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

SBS — Side by Side Pours collects no personal data. Nothing leaves your
phone unless you choose to share it.

## What SBS uses

- **Bluetooth**, only to connect to your coffee scales and read their
  weight, flow, battery and timer.
- **Storage on your phone**, to keep your saved runs and remember which
  scale sits in which slot. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What SBS doesn't do

- No accounts, sign-in or servers.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No location, contacts, photos or microphone.

## Sharing

When you export a run as CSV, iOS shows its share sheet and you choose
where it goes. SBS sends it nowhere on its own.

## Children

SBS collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
effective date.
