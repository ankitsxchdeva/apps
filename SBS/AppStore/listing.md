## Description (4000)

SBS records two to four pours at the same time, one Bluetooth coffee scale each, and shows how closely they track.

Practising your pour, comparing kettles, or teaching someone else? Put a scale under each brewer, start pouring, and watch both curves build live. When you stop, SBS scores how well each pour matched the first and lines up final weight, pour time, average and peak flow.

• Live weight curves for every scale on one chart
• Each scale is timed by its own timer, so you can start the second pour a few seconds after the first
• A match score for every pour against scale A
• Accidental taps on a scale are smoothed out automatically
• Up to four scales at once
• Save runs with a note, and export any run as CSV
• Try it without scales using the built-in demo

Works with Bookoo Themis scales. Acaia, Felicita, Decent, Varia AKU and DiFluid scales are supported in beta.

No accounts, no tracking, no data collected.

## Notes for App Review

SBS needs Bluetooth coffee scales, so it includes a demo mode for review:

1. Tap the ? button in the top right.
2. Tap "Try with demo scales". Four simulated scales connect.
3. Tap "Tare and reset all". The demo scales start their timers one after another and pour.
4. Tap "Stop" to see the results screen: match scores, stats and charts. Save or discard.
5. Tap "End demo" to return to real scales.

With real scales, the app connects over Bluetooth to Bookoo Themis scales (fully supported) and, in beta, Acaia, Felicita, Decent, Varia and DiFluid scales. No account or sign-in is needed.
